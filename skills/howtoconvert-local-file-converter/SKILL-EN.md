## 🎯 Module 2: Core Value Proposition & Arbitrage Wedge

### Market Friction & User Pain Points
Millions search how to convert one format to another, but online converters upload files to unknown servers with ads and missing formats, while command-line tools are unusable for most people. Archivists, legal, medical and finance staff are often banned from uploading work files at all; the original's reviews include a digital archivist seeking a workplace license. The product is literally named after the search query, each format pair gets an SEO page (124.9K monthly visits), the free in-browser version funnels users to a $29 offline license, and server costs are zero. Copycats should skip the mass market and serve one no-upload profession with its 20-30 frequent formats, batch mode and audit logs.

### Opportunity & Unfair Arbitrage
No-upload privacy is a real wedge and the search-query naming playbook is copyable, but one-time pricing has no recurring revenue and the original is down to $417/month; copycats must narrow to professionals who are banned from uploading files.
