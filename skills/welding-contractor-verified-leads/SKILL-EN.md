## 🎯 Module 2: Core Value Proposition & Arbitrage Wedge

### Market Friction & User Pain Points
Welding and fabrication contractors do not lack skill; they lack a steady flow of commercial and industrial jobs. They rely on word of mouth and phone books, and the project tips they get are often stale or already taken. Fifteen verified real projects a month for $330 a quarter is a rounding error against a single job.

### Opportunity & Unfair Arbitrage
The reference business launched in June 2026 and reached 46 paying members, $32,093 in revenue, $29,443 in profit and a 91.74% margin in five months, delivering 15 research-verified leads per member per month (Microns listing, November 3, 2026). The stack is Notion + WordPress + Airtable + Google Sheets, essentially no-code; acquisition runs through a 48,000-member welding Facebook community, and the owner spends under 5 hours a week. The moat is not technology, it is the reputation that every lead was actually verified.

## 🚀 Module 3: 4-Phase Turnkey Execution SOP

### Phase 01 · Pick one trade, then build a 500-record buyer list [Reverse Insight]
- **Action:** Do not cover every industry at once; start with welding/fabrication only. Hand-collect 500 real commercial project records from public tenders, site notices and local business filings (project name, location, contact, start date), each verifiable by a phone call.
- **Deliverable:** 500 human-verified buyer project records

### Phase 02 · One landing page plus emailed leads is enough to open [Rapid Build]
- **Action:** A WordPress landing page explaining '15 verified leads a month', Airtable for the lead database, Stripe for quarterly/annual billing, and leads delivered by email in monthly batches. Do not build a platform first; run delivery on spreadsheets plus email.
- **Deliverable:** Minimal version that can take payment and deliver leads

### Phase 03 · $330/quarter or $987/year, first 10 seed members [Commercialization]
- **Action:** Copy the reference pricing: $330/quarter, $987/year. Recruit 10 seed members in welding Facebook groups and local trade groups at half price for the first 3 months, in exchange for monthly feedback on which leads actually turned into jobs; use those closed-deal stories as the renewal pitch.
- **Deliverable:** 10 seed paying members + first month of lead feedback

### Phase 04 · Own the welding community + member referrals [Cold Outbound]
- **Action:** The main channel is the welding Facebook community: post one anonymized sample of that week's verified leads every week, no hard selling; add a referral reward (one free month per referred member). Lead quality is the renewal engine, so publish closed-deal feedback every month.
- **Deliverable:** A subscription base on the order of 46 paying members

## 🛡️ Module 5: Anti-Pitfall Checklist & Compliance Guardrails

- [ ] **Critical Warning**: 1) Every lead must be human-verified; one fake lead loses one member, and the 22.7% September churn rate is the lesson. 2) Only use public tenders and business filings; never scrape private phone numbers or emails. 3) Never promise guaranteed jobs; you sell leads, not orders, and closing is the contractor's job. 4) Do not expand to ten trades at once; earn a reputation in one trade first, then copy the playbook to the next trade, such as electricians or HVAC.
- [ ] **Validation Gate**: Secure first commitment or preorder before over-engineering complex backend features.
- [ ] **Churn Defense**: Keep turnaround time fast and maintain direct founder communication with initial buyers.
