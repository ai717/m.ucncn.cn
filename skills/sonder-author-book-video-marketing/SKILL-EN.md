## 🎯 Module 2: Core Value Proposition & Arbitrage Wedge

### Market Friction & User Pain Points
Authors do not struggle to write books; they struggle to be seen after writing one. An agency charges $750+/month for five videos plus briefing calls, which most indie authors cannot afford, and editing it themselves takes time they do not have. The book is finished, the marketing videos are blank, and that gap is the paying point.

### Opportunity & Unfair Arbitrage
Sonder launched September 10, 2026 and reached about $20,283 MRR with 113 subscriptions in roughly two months, with $18,374 verified over the last 30 days (TrustMRR, November 2, 2026). The play is not generation quality but a library of proven script structures reverse-engineered from thousands of book videos that actually sold: hook, tension, reveal. At $49/month against an agency's $750+, the price gap itself is the pitch.

## 🚀 Module 3: 4-Phase Turnkey Execution SOP

### Phase 01 · Collect 50 viral book-video script structures first [Reverse Insight]
- **Action:** Pull 50 book videos on TikTok/Instagram that actually drove sales, break each into hook, tension, reveal and ending, and distill 10 reusable script templates. The templates are the moat; build them before writing code.
- **Deliverable:** 10 book-marketing script templates + viral structure breakdown

### Phase 02 · PDF upload, read the whole book, output scripts, render video [Rapid Build]
- **Action:** Ship one pipeline only for the MVP: upload PDF and cover, LLM reads the whole book and pulls arguments and stories, applies the script templates to produce 3 video scripts, then a video generation API renders captioned clips. Add slideshows and Amazon ad formats only after this works.
- **Deliverable:** Working book-to-video pipeline

### Phase 03 · 7-day free trial, then $49/month and up [Commercialization]
- **Action:** Full access free for 7 days, then $49/month from day 8; three tiers at $49/$99/$209 by monthly video count, with $35+ credit packs for overages. Stripe billing, and price per video only after costing each render, so heavy users cannot burn the margin.
- **Deliverable:** Stripe subscriptions + per-video costed pricing table

### Phase 04 · Author communities + writing creators on commission [Cold Outbound]
- **Action:** Give 20 authors a free month in author communities, self-publishing forums and through writing creators, in exchange for posting their videos with a link; run content on the '$49 for 5 videos vs $750+ at an agency' price contrast.
- **Deliverable:** First 10-50 paying subscribers

## 🛡️ Module 5: Anti-Pitfall Checklist & Compliance Guardrails

- [ ] **Critical Warning**: 1) Do not compete on generation quality alone; Kling/Runway/Sora upgrades will erase that edge. The moat is script patterns plus the author community. 2) Cost every video before pricing; heavy users can eat the margin if pricing is flat. 3) Never train public models on authors' book content; one copyright dispute is fatal. 4) Never promise views or sales; ad results depend on the book and the ad budget, the platform only guarantees the videos.
- [ ] **Validation Gate**: Secure first commitment or preorder before over-engineering complex backend features.
- [ ] **Churn Defense**: Keep turnaround time fast and maintain direct founder communication with initial buyers.
