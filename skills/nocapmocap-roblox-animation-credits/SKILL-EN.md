## 🎯 Module 2: Core Value Proposition & Arbitrage Wedge

### Market Friction & User Pain Points
Roblox indie developers need dozens of character animations per game, but hand-animating is slow and specialized, stock packs are expensive and overused (the founder calls it the Mixamo loop), and mocap suits are out of reach. The tool turns a text prompt or a phone video into R15 animation in seconds, previews it inside a free Studio plugin, sells prepaid credits that never expire ($20 for 100 credits), and even lets coding agents generate animations through an API. Copycats can target Unity, Godot or UEFN, or own one Roblox genre such as dance, fighting or parkour moves.

### Opportunity & Unfair Arbitrage
Roblox creators genuinely lack animations and credit-pack cash flow is real, but motion quality and GPU cost are hard gates; it passes on the line and suits builders with 3D or motion expertise.

## 🚀 Module 3: 4-Phase Turnkey Execution SOP

### Phase 01: Execution Step
- **Action:** Pick one engine (Roblox R15, Unity Humanoid or Godot) and one high-frequency move family; collect 50 animations target users actually ship and confirm skeleton and export standards.

### Phase 02: Execution Step
- **Action:** Build the pipeline: text-to-motion via open models or APIs, video via pose estimation plus retargeting; ship web generation with file export first, then an in-editor preview plugin. Quality floor: no foot sliding, no mesh clipping.

### Phase 03: Execution Step
- **Action:** Sell prepaid credit packs (never expire) plus monthly plan credits (expire monthly); quote the credit price before generating, bake GPU cost into pricing, and auto-refund failed generations.

### Phase 04: Execution Step

## 🛡️ Module 5: Anti-Pitfall Checklist & Compliance Guardrails

- [ ] **Critical Warning**: Motion quality is life or death: foot sliding, clipping and broken skeletons churn users instantly, so test inside real target games. Video mocap raises likeness and choreography rights issues; require users to only upload video they have rights to. GPU cost scales with generations, so price credits with margin and per-user caps. If the platform ships native AI animation, differentiate with vertical move libraries and workflow integration. Never-expiring credits are a liability; reserve compute budget for them.
- [ ] **Validation Gate**: Secure first commitment or preorder before over-engineering complex backend features.
- [ ] **Churn Defense**: Keep turnaround time fast and maintain direct founder communication with initial buyers.
