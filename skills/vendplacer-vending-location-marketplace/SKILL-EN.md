## 🎯 Module 2: Core Value Proposition & Arbitrage Wedge

### Market Friction & User Pain Points
Independent vending operators struggle most with finding high-traffic placements; brokers charge high fees and cold-calling businesses is slow. Owners of malls, offices and gyms have idle corners worth $48-$150/month in rent but no channel to reach operators. The original lists 22,876 locations across 420 cities. The platform sells contact access for a flat annual fee, never touches rent, and uses programmatic city and venue SEO pages for traffic. Copycats should seed supply in one city cluster first, then sell annual plans to local operator communities; the same playbook extends to EV chargers, ATMs and claw machines.

### Opportunity & Unfair Arbitrage
Location hunting is the top bottleneck for vending operators and willingness to pay is proven, but marketplace cold start is hard: copycats must win one city cluster at a time.
