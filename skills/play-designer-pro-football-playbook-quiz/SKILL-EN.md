## 🎯 Module 2: Core Value Proposition & Arbitrage Wedge

### Market Friction & User Pain Points
Coaches redraw paper plays for every change and cannot verify whether players learned assignments. Structured formations and responsibilities let one drawing become a testable team assignment, and share links turn each coach into a team-wide channel.

### Opportunity & Unfair Arbitrage
