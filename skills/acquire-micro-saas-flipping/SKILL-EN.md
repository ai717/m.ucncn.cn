## 💻 Module 3: Clean Modular Asset Isolation Architecture

To ensure a frictionless asset sale on Acquire.com, enforce strict isolation from Day 1:
1. **Domain & DNS**: Dedicated domain on a standalone Cloudflare or Namecheap account ready for instant transfer;
2. **Database & Auth**: Isolated Supabase project; never co-mingle multiple apps in one Supabase database;
3. **Billing**: Dedicated Stripe Sub-account or separate LemonSqueezy store ready for business ownership transfer;
4. **Git Cleanliness**: Zero hardcoded secrets in Git history; strictly inject credentials via `.env` variables.

## 🤝 Module 5: Escrow.com Closing Pipeline

1. **Sign LOI (Letter of Intent)**: Lock terms and 14-day exclusivity period;
2. **Open Escrow**: Buyer deposits 100% of purchase funds into Escrow.com;
3. **Asset Handover**: Transfer domain DNS, assign GitHub repository owner permissions, and migrate Stripe customer tokens;
4. **Fund Release**: Buyer signs inspection acceptance; Escrow releases cash directly to your bank account.
