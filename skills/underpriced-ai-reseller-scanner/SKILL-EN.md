## 🎯 Module 2: Core Value Proposition & Arbitrage Wedge

### Market Friction & User Pain Points
Resellers deciding on the spot at thrift stores and garage sales fear overpaying most. The old way took 10-15 minutes per item: Google Lens, asking ChatGPT, digging through eBay sold listings, then hand-calculating the 13.25% fee and shipping. By the time the math was done, someone else had taken the good item. Founder Frank Kratzer was a reseller himself and built it solo in 6 months while learning AI integration.

### Opportunity & Unfair Arbitrage
Compress 'identify + check sold prices + compute net profit' into 30 seconds: photograph the item, a vision model identifies brand, model and condition, real eBay sold prices anchor the value, then platform fees and shipping are deducted to produce a 0-100 Deal Score and a buy-or-skip call. One good flip (a $12 item netting $80) pays for a year of subscription, so the purchase decision is easy. Indie Hackers shows $5,156/mo Stripe-verified (29 October 2026), rechecked at $5,236.94 per 30 days on 7 November 2026. Live on web, iOS, Android and Chrome/Firefox extensions.

## 🚀 Module 3: 4-Phase Turnkey Execution SOP

### Phase 01 · Live inside reseller communities and confirm the 30-second on-the-spot decision is a real pain [Reverse Insight]
- **Action:** Spend two weeks in r/Flipping, Facebook reseller groups and eBay seller forums. Record how sellers actually decide on site, what they complain about, which categories trip them up most, what tool stack they use now, and what they would pay per scan. Pick target categories (vintage cameras, designer jackets, sneakers, collectibles) before building; do not attempt all categories.
- **Deliverable:** Target category list + 5 seller interviews + willingness-to-pay range

### Phase 02 · Photo identification + eBay sold prices + net-profit calculator working [Rapid Build]
- **Action:** Use a cheap vision model (e.g. Gemini Flash) to identify brand, model and condition. Pull real sold prices through the official eBay Finding API as the anchor (never scrape). Deduct platform fees from a rate table (eBay 13.25% + $0.30, Poshmark 20%, Mercari ~12.9%) plus shipping to compute net profit and a Deal Score. Ship a web MVP first and verify identification accuracy before wrapping an app.
- **Deliverable:** Web MVP + measured identification accuracy + net-profit calculator passing a 20-item physical spot check

### Phase 03 · 5 free scans to hook, three subscription tiers, first month half price [Commercialization]
- **Action:** Give 5 full free scans (including sold-price comparison, no paywall on the key answer) so users taste one 'good find' first. Paid tiers scale by scan volume (Reseller $9.99/mo for 75 scans, Pro $14.99/mo for 200, Ultra $24.99/mo for 500), first month half price to lower the barrier. Bill through Stripe. If the free tier gets farmed, tighten it to 5 scans and do not soften.
- **Deliverable:** Stripe subscription loop + pricing page + free-tier abuse rules

### Phase 04 · Reseller communities + Product Hunt + free tools for traffic [Cold Outbound]
- **Action:** Post real flip stories in reseller communities (how a $15 camera was calculated to net $87), not ads. Launch on Product Hunt for a visibility spike. Build 40+ free mini-tools (fee calculators, shipping estimators, ROI calculators) for search traffic. Anchor word of mouth on one line: 'one scan pays for the subscription'. Let existing users bring new ones.
- **Deliverable:** 10 community case posts + Product Hunt launch + free-tool matrix

## 🛡️ Module 5: Anti-Pitfall Checklist & Compliance Guardrails

- [ ] **Critical Warning**: A valuation is an estimate, not an appraisal: state clearly on the page that AI valuations can be wrong and that condition and authenticity are the user's call. Never promise 'guaranteed authentic / guaranteed price', or disputes will follow. The eBay Finding API is official but rate-limited; do not work around it with scraping or an account ban ends everything. Pricing note: the founder's January 2026 post lists $5.99/$9.99/$17.99, while the October 2026 Product Hunt update lists $9.99/$14.99/$24.99; use the latest. Low price means volume: 500 subscriptions make $5,000/mo, so keep acquisition costs down and do not burn money on ads.
- [ ] **Validation Gate**: Secure first commitment or preorder before over-engineering complex backend features.
- [ ] **Churn Defense**: Keep turnaround time fast and maintain direct founder communication with initial buyers.
