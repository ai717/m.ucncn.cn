## 🎯 Module 2: Core Value Proposition & Arbitrage Wedge

### Market Friction & User Pain Points
Local trades such as roofers win jobs through local search and word of mouth but often lack a credible website. Agencies charge thousands upfront with abandonment risk, and DIY builders demand time owners do not have. The wedge is to flip the sale: build the site first for free, show it in a 2-minute video, and charge $199/mo only after the owner approves going live. Hosting and DNS stay in the operator account, creating switching friction, while the monthly fee buys ongoing maintenance rather than a one-off build. At roughly 1% email conversion, about 200 personalized emails produced the first 3 clients and 2,000-2,500 emails produced 23 signings.

### Opportunity & Unfair Arbitrage
A concrete niche with a friction-free offer: zero upfront cost removes the small merchant fear of an unfinished build. Evidence is C-grade self-reporting, cold-email conversion is only about 1%, and 17% of clients churned, so replicators must win on personalization and maintenance discipline. Tier C: validate small before scaling client count.

- **Monetization Model**: No build fee + $199/mo hosting and maintenance subscription (SSL, backups, capped monthly edits), upsells billed separately

## 🚀 Module 3: 4-Phase Turnkey Execution SOP

### Phase 01: Execution Step
- **Action:** Pick one trade and one metro area, list 200 businesses with reviews but no site or a clearly outdated site, and rank them by review activity and site gap.

### Phase 02: Execution Step
- **Action:** Generate a custom one-page site per prospect from its own public business facts, with a call button, services, reviews, service area and contact form, plus a 2-minute walkthrough video.

### Phase 03: Execution Step
- **Action:** Send one-to-one personalized emails with the preview link, clear $199/mo scope, edit caps, cancellation terms, real sender identity and opt-out; sign an e-agreement and start Stripe billing on approval.

### Phase 04: Execution Step
- **Action:** Enforce per-client edit caps and response times, log maintenance hours per client, sell extra pages and review-collection add-ons as upsells, and review churn monthly before expanding to a second trade or city.

## 🛡️ Module 5: Anti-Pitfall Checklist & Compliance Guardrails

- [ ] **Critical Warning**: Cold email must stay one-to-one and personalized with real identity, postal address and opt-out; bought lists and blast sending will burn domain reputation. Never fabricate reviews, credentials or service promises on demo sites. The $199/mo plan must cap included edits and response times or maintenance creep will erase the margin. Put domain ownership and migration terms in the agreement to avoid hostage-domain disputes.
