People who want to publish an ebook stall on the grind: topic research, outline, writing, layout and export each need a different tool and hours of time. Marketers and small business owners have material worth writing but never ship because writing a book feels too heavy, and generic AI writing tools only return scattered paragraphs, not a finished book to sell. gettbook.ai packages the whole chain: research, outline, writing, layout and export from a single idea. ## 🚀 Module 3: 4-Phase Turnkey Execution SOP

### Phase 01: Execution Step
- **Action:** Lock in one audience that pays: marketers and small business owners. Interview 10 people who want an ebook for lead generation or sales, map how they publish today and what they would pay, and mine competing AI writing tools for the gap: paragraphs but no finished book.

### Phase 02: Execution Step
- **Action:** Ship an MVP in two weeks with vibe coding. Idea in, research, editable outline, chapter-by-chapter writing, layout and PDF/EPUB export out; nail one book type (how-to guides) before adding more; the included ebook_builder.py script runs outline generation and sample-chapter export; write tests from day one so the codebase never needs a full rebuild.

### Phase 03: Execution Step
- **Action:** Close the commercial loop. Subscriptions from $29/mo, usage credit packs and tutorial upsells to thicken average order value, Stripe for subscriptions and one-off upsells, and a weekly true CAC and margin review that tightens ad spend the moment it passes 60% of revenue.

### Phase 04: Execution Step
- **Action:** Cold-start with Meta ads in small fast tests. Spend $500 testing 10 creatives, scale only the one whose CAC sits below one third of order value, instrument every funnel step (click, signup, trial, paid), refresh creatives weekly against fatigue, and publish free sample chapters on YouTube and communities for organic traffic that hedges ad dependence.
