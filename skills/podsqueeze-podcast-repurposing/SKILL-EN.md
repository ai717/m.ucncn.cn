## 🎯 Module 2: Core Value Proposition & Arbitrage Wedge

### Market Friction & User Pain Points
Recording is only half the work: transcripts, notes, timestamps, titles, newsletters, posts and clips eat hours per episode. One upload that returns the full distribution pack, priced by audio minutes, keeps margins above 80%. Specialize in one show format instead of matching every feature.
