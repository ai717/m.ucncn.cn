## 🎯 Module 2: Core Value Proposition & Arbitrage Wedge

### Market Friction & User Pain Points
Small repair shops still run on paper tickets, spreadsheets and generic invoicing apps: intake, quotes, parts stock, pickup notifications and invoicing live in separate places, causing lost jobs and constant status calls. Generic ERPs are heavy and expensive. The original serves 70 shops at about $2,931 MRR by wiring the full repair flow into one line and baking local rules into the product (NF525, 2026 e-invoicing, police ledger, FEC exports), turning compliance into the reason to buy. Copycats should pick one country and one trade, master its invoicing and ledger rules, and win shops one by one through demos and referrals; once ticket and stock data live in the system, renewal is sticky.

### Opportunity & Unfair Arbitrage
Repair-shop digitization is a real need and built-in compliance is a real moat, but B2B sales cycles are long and compliance must be rebuilt per country; it suits builders willing to sell shop by shop.

## 🚀 Module 3: 4-Phase Turnkey Execution SOP

### Phase 01: Execution Step
- **Action:** Pick one country and one trade (e.g. phone repair), shadow 5-10 shops for a day, and map intake, quote approval, parts and invoicing rules into one flow diagram.

### Phase 02: Execution Step
- **Action:** Ship the core four: work orders with status flow, quotes with customer approval links, compliant invoices, and parts stock with auto-deduction, plus pickup SMS notifications.

### Phase 03: Execution Step
- **Action:** Price from EUR 19/month with a 14-day trial; import the shop's customer and parts data during trial so the data itself locks them in; add multi-store tiers later.

### Phase 04: Execution Step
- **Action:** Cold-start offline: in-person demos, parts-wholesaler referrals and trade-group word of mouth; win 20 flagship shops in one city, then expand by referral to 50-80.

## 🛡️ Module 5: Anti-Pitfall Checklist & Compliance Guardrails

- [ ] **Critical Warning**: Compliance is both moat and liability: invoicing, ledgers and tax exports must track current local law, and errors can trigger claims. Customer phone numbers and repair records are sensitive data; back up and isolate per-shop access. Do not launch multi-country or multi-trade; fields and rules differ per trade. Avoid promising done-for-you services beyond the software.
- [ ] **Validation Gate**: Secure first commitment or preorder before over-engineering complex backend features.
- [ ] **Churn Defense**: Keep turnaround time fast and maintain direct founder communication with initial buyers.
