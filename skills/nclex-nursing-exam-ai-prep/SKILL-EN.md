## 🎯 Module 2: Core Value Proposition & Arbitrage Wedge

### Market Friction & User Pain Points
Hundreds of thousands of US nursing students sit the NCLEX every year, and failing means no license and no job. Existing prep is either a several-hundred-dollar course or scattered free question sets, while the lecture notes students already own sit unused. Students will pay directly for 'turn my notes into exam questions'.

## 🚀 Module 3: 4-Phase Turnkey Execution SOP

### Phase 01 · One exam only: ship 200 human-checked questions first [Reverse Insight]
- **Action:** Cover NCLEX-RN only, nothing else. Map the official Client Needs categories into a high-frequency topic list, then hand-build 200 multiple-choice questions with explanations as the seed bank. Every AI-generated question passes human review first; accuracy is the whole business.
- **Deliverable:** NCLEX topic map + 200 human-checked seed questions

### Phase 02 · Notes-to-question-bank site with a scored self-test [Rapid Build]
- **Action:** Ship a minimal FastAPI + static frontend build: upload PDF/notes, LLM extracts topics and generates flashcards and quizzes, then online self-testing, a mistake log and a readiness score. Get 'upload, generate, practice, score' working end to end before adding an AI tutor chat.
- **Deliverable:** Working notes-to-test site with a scoring engine

### Phase 03 · Free practice tier + $29-$59 monthly subscription [Commercialization]
- **Action:** Free tier allows 20 questions a day; paid unlocks the full bank, mock exams and explanations. Price against the reference ARPU of $56.65 with a $39/mo basic tier and a $59/mo sprint tier. Stripe subscriptions, cancel anytime, no forced annual lock-in.
- **Deliverable:** Stripe subscription checkout + two-tier pricing page

### Phase 04 · Nursing student creators + nursing school partnerships [Cold Outbound]
- **Action:** Recruit 5-10 nursing students as short-form video creators on a revenue share; the content is simply 'my lecture notes became a mock exam in 10 minutes'. Offer free classroom trial accounts to nursing schools and use real pass rates as social proof.
- **Deliverable:** First 10-50 paying subscribers

## 🛡️ Module 5: Anti-Pitfall Checklist & Compliance Guardrails

- [ ] **Critical Warning**: 1) Every AI-generated exam question must be human-checked; wrong questions destroy trust, and medical content must never ship straight from a model. 2) Do not resell copied real exam sets; original rewrites plus explanations are the compliant route. 3) Never promise a guaranteed pass; a refund-on-failure offer is a hole you cannot close. 4) Notes belong to the student; only process materials users upload themselves, never scrape or resell question banks.
- [ ] **Validation Gate**: Secure first commitment or preorder before over-engineering complex backend features.
- [ ] **Churn Defense**: Keep turnaround time fast and maintain direct founder communication with initial buyers.
