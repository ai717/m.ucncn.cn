## 🎯 Module 2: Core Value Proposition & Arbitrage Wedge

### Market Friction & User Pain Points
Heavy AI users spread chats across four platforms with no unified search, folders or prompt reuse, and no vendor will index a rival's history. A local-only extension owns that cross-platform layer at near-zero running cost, so lifetime pricing works. Unlike generic extension plays, this does one job across four platforms.

### Opportunity & Unfair Arbitrage
Best evidence quality in this batch (TrustMRR verified), but revenue is lifetime-heavy and platform changes can break it; a solid C-tier niche play.
