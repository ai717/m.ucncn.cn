## 🎯 Module 2: Core Value Proposition & Arbitrage Wedge

### Market Friction & User Pain Points
Tattoo artists spend about an hour hand-tracing each client photo into a transferable stencil, and in peak season that hour is a lost booking. Generic image generators produce pretty pictures, not clean, true-size, print-ready line art. A tool that does only photo-to-stencil well, with adjustable line weight and centimeter-accurate PDF export, priced by credits with a $0.99 trial pack, fits the workflow. Copycats should specialize in one style such as realism portraits.

### Opportunity & Unfair Arbitrage
A sharply defined buyer paying to save an hour per job, with TrustMRR-verified revenue; the incumbent has been flat around $60K-$64K/month since July 2026, so copycats must win on line quality or the sizing workflow. B tier.

## 🚀 Module 3: 4-Phase Turnkey Execution SOP

### Phase 01: Execution Step
- **Action:** Interview 10 working tattoo artists and test stencil quality on their real photo samples until at least 8 say the output can go straight to the transfer printer.

### Phase 02: Execution Step
- **Action:** Build the pipeline: grayscale, contrast, edge extraction and binarization, exporting a line-art PNG plus a true-size PDF at 300 dpi with two sliders for line weight and detail.

### Phase 03: Execution Step
