## 🎯 Module 2: Core Value Proposition & Arbitrage Wedge

### Market Friction & User Pain Points
Creators waste close to an hour a day copying the same video across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and X, each with different format, title and cover rules, while existing schedulers are expensive and bloated for small creators. Post Bridge does exactly one small job well: one dashboard, multi-platform OAuth, automatic per-platform adaptation, and simple per-account pricing instead of per-post billing. A hard paywall from day one filters for people willing to pay. Packaging distribution as a REST API, CLI and MCP server lets AI assistants publish on the user's behalf, riding the 2026 agent-workflow wave. The founder built it for his own daily marketing, so the product matched a real pain from the start.

### Opportunity & Unfair Arbitrage
63 points, Tier C (60-69, worth watching), above the 60-point bar. Verified first-hand against the IndieFounder story (published 2026-11-14): TrustMRR via Stripe shows ~$48,500 MRR, ~$582,000 all-time revenue and ~1,600 subscriptions; Marketer plan from $39/mo for 15 accounts with unlimited posts on paid plans; solo founder Jack Friks runs it with infra under $2,000/mo and 90%+ gross margins; he declined a $1M acquisition offer in spring 2025; signups spiked after the ChatGPT plugin and Claude connector listings in September 2026. Watch out: distribution depends almost entirely on X build-in-public, platform API policies (Meta/TikTok/Instagram) can tighten at any time, and support plus development rest on one person. ## 🚀 Module 3: 4-Phase Turnkey Execution SOP

### Phase 01: Execution Step
- **Action:** Validate on your own accounts first. Post manually to 3-5 platforms daily for 30 days, log the time and format traps, interview 10 multi-platform creators about willingness to pay, and lock in one audience: short-form creators and solo founders.

### Phase 02: Execution Step
- **Action:** Ship an MVP in about a month. Supabase plus Vercel plus Trigger.dev plus Unkey keeps monthly infra under $2,000; connect X, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube OAuth and posting APIs first; adapt titles and covers per platform (the included postbridge_publish.py script runs caption adaptation and publish-queue checks); launch behind a hard paywall with Stripe per-account billing.

### Phase 03: Execution Step
- **Action:** Close the commercial loop. Price per connected account (Marketer from $39/mo, 15 accounts), unlimited posts on paid plans, then add REST API, npm CLI and MCP server so Claude and ChatGPT can schedule and publish for users, with usage tied to account count to stop abuse.

### Phase 04: Execution Step
- **Action:** Cold-start distribution. Build in public on X with real revenue updates, post short-form video daily from your own accounts, launch on Product Hunt and Indie Hackers, and give the first 100 paying users a founder price in exchange for testimonials and renewals.

## 🛡️ Module 5: Anti-Pitfall Checklist & Compliance Guardrails

- [ ] **Critical Warning**: Distribution leans almost entirely on X, so an algorithm change or account problem stalls growth at once; build email and short-video backup channels from day one. Platform API policies (Meta, TikTok, Instagram) can tighten or reprice; failed posts need visible fallback and a retry queue, never silent drops. Solo operation means support, development and ops sit on one person; fix support flows before adding features once subscriptions grow. The ~1,600 subscriptions and 29,000+ signups are founder-reported snapshots; quote them with date and source, and re-check TrustMRR live figures before quoting.
- [ ] **Validation Gate**: Secure first commitment or preorder before over-engineering complex backend features.
- [ ] **Churn Defense**: Keep turnaround time fast and maintain direct founder communication with initial buyers.
