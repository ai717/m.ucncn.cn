🔍 Market Opportunity & Founder Bottlenecks

Local trades such as roofers win jobs through local search and word of mouth but often lack a credible website. Agencies charge thousands upfront with abandonment risk, and DIY builders demand time owners do not have. The wedge is to flip the sale: build the site first for free, show it in a 2-minute video, and charge $199/mo only after the owner approves going live. Hosting and DNS stay in the operator account, creating switching friction, while the monthly fee buys ongoing maintenance rather than a one-off build. At roughly 1% email conversion, about 200 personalized emails produced the first 3 clients and 2,000-2,500 emails produced 23 signings.

🛠️ 4-Step Operational Blueprint (SOP)

➔ Phase 01: Pick one trade and one metro area, list 200 businesses with reviews but no site or a clearly outdated site, and rank them by review activity and site gap.
➔ Phase 02: Generate a custom one-page site per prospect from its own public business facts, with a call button, services, reviews, service area and contact form, plus a 2-minute walkthrough video.
➔ Phase 03: Send one-to-one personalized emails with the preview link, clear $199/mo scope, edit caps, cancellation terms, real sender identity and opt-out; sign an e-agreement and start Stripe billing on approval.
➔ Phase 04: Enforce per-client edit caps and response times, log maintenance hours per client, sell extra pages and review-collection add-ons as upsells, and review churn monthly before expanding to a second trade or city.

⚠️ Pitfalls & Risk Warning

Cold email must stay one-to-one and personalized with real identity, postal address and opt-out; bought lists and blast sending will burn domain reputation. Never fabricate reviews, credentials or service promises on demo sites. The $199/mo plan must cap included edits and response times or maintenance creep will erase the margin. Put domain ownership and migration terms in the agreement to avoid hostage-domain disputes. Treat the benchmark $4,400 MRR as self-reported revenue including undisclosed upsells and budget on a $1,500-$3,500 replicator range.
