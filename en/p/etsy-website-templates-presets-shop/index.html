Etsy Website Template and Preset Shop: Ready-Made Themes for Small Shops at $60-$160

Sell Shopify, Wix and Squarespace website templates ($60-$160) and Lightroom preset packs ($14) on Etsy to small fashion, beauty and wellness shops as instant downloads with no inventory or shipping. The benchmark shop opened in 2022, sold 5,815 orders across 89 templates for about $297K lifetime revenue, and made about $20,800 in the last 12 months after adding only 3 new listings in 2026.

🔍 Market Opportunity & Founder Bottlenecks

Small fashion, beauty and wellness sellers want their own online shop but stall on design and technical skills: a freelance designer costs hundreds to thousands of dollars, big theme marketplaces feel generic, and default themes look cheap. They pay for a ready-made theme that already looks like a leading shop in their niche, with a setup guide, so they can swap photos and text and start selling. Building per-niche templates and winning Etsy long-tail search terms keeps the ticket at $60-$160 with zero marginal cost, while preset packs add a $14 attach item. The benchmark also shows the failure mode: revenue fell from GBP 62,615 in 2022 to GBP 12,395 in the first ten months of 2026 after only 3 new listings that year, so steady new releases are the lifeline.

🛠️ 4-Step Operational Blueprint (SOP)

➔ Phase 01: Pick one visual niche such as beauty, weddings or yoga, study top Etsy template shops, their prices and review complaints, and list 5 template ideas with long-tail keywords before building.
➔ Phase 02: Design in Figma and build Shopify themes to Online Store 2.0 standards in Liquid, HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with Wix and Squarespace variants, setup guides, preview images and demo videos; ship the first 5-8 templates.
➔ Phase 03: List on Etsy at $60-$160 per website template with $14 preset packs as add-ons, fill titles and all 13 tags with niche long-tail terms, and run the listing checker for title, tags and net payout before publishing.
➔ Phase 04: Release at least 2-3 new templates or variants every month and expand to your own website, Pinterest and template marketplaces such as Creative Market.

⚠️ Pitfalls & Risk Warning

An Etsy shop account, its reviews and search ranking cannot be transferred, so a fresh shop may earn near zero for the first months; validate with a small catalogue first. Themes must track Shopify and platform standards or they get delisted and down-rated. Use only owned or commercially licensed photos and fonts, since infringement is the most common takedown cause for download shops. Treat the benchmark decline as real and plan on $500-$1,500 per month, not the $297K lifetime figure.
