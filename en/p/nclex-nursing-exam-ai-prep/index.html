⚡ Institutional Due Diligence Verdict

Licensure exams recur every year, and 915 paying subscribers with $18.8K MRR prove students really pay. A solo builder can ship the question bank with AI; the main risk is that AI-written questions must be human-checked for accuracy.

🔍 Market Opportunity & Founder Bottlenecks

【Market Pain & Gap】Hundreds of thousands of US nursing students sit the NCLEX every year, and failing means no license and no job. Existing prep is either a several-hundred-dollar course or scattered free question sets, while the lecture notes students already own sit unused. Students will pay directly for 'turn my notes into exam questions'.
【Opportunity & Arbitrage】The reference product's verified DealStream listing shows 915 paying subscribers, $18,824 MRR, $56.65 ARPU and an 89.6% gross margin, with payment data connected and verified. The wedge is one exam only (NCLEX), so the question bank and syllabus stay tightly focused; an LLM converts notes into flashcards and quizzes at token cost only; acquisition runs through short-form video made with nursing student creators, and the founder spends 15-20 hours a week on operations.

🛠️ 4-Step Operational Blueprint (SOP)

Phase 01 · One exam only: ship 200 human-checked questions first Reverse Insight

Cover NCLEX-RN only, nothing else. Map the official Client Needs categories into a high-frequency topic list, then hand-build 200 multiple-choice questions with explanations as the seed bank. Every AI-generated question passes human review first; accuracy is the whole business.

🏁 Deliverable:NCLEX topic map + 200 human-checked seed questions
Phase 02 · Notes-to-question-bank site with a scored self-test Rapid Build

Ship a minimal FastAPI + static frontend build: upload PDF/notes, LLM extracts topics and generates flashcards and quizzes, then online self-testing, a mistake log and a readiness score. Get 'upload, generate, practice, score' working end to end before adding an AI tutor chat.

🏁 Deliverable:Working notes-to-test site with a scoring engine
Phase 03 · Free practice tier + $29-$59 monthly subscription Commercialization

Free tier allows 20 questions a day; paid unlocks the full bank, mock exams and explanations. Price against the reference ARPU of $56.65 with a $39/mo basic tier and a $59/mo sprint tier. Stripe subscriptions, cancel anytime, no forced annual lock-in.

🏁 Deliverable:Stripe subscription checkout + two-tier pricing page
Phase 04 · Nursing student creators + nursing school partnerships Cold Outbound

Recruit 5-10 nursing students as short-form video creators on a revenue share; the content is simply 'my lecture notes became a mock exam in 10 minutes'. Offer free classroom trial accounts to nursing schools and use real pass rates as social proof.

🏁 Deliverable:First 10-50 paying subscribers

⚠️ Pitfalls & Risk Warning

1) Every AI-generated exam question must be human-checked; wrong questions destroy trust, and medical content must never ship straight from a model. 2) Do not resell copied real exam sets; original rewrites plus explanations are the compliant route. 3) Never promise a guaranteed pass; a refund-on-failure offer is a hole you cannot close. 4) Notes belong to the student; only process materials users upload themselves, never scrape or resell question banks.
