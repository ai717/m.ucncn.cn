🔍 Market Opportunity & Founder Bottlenecks

Creators waste close to an hour a day copying the same video across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and X, each with different format, title and cover rules, while existing schedulers are expensive and bloated for small creators. Post Bridge does exactly one small job well: one dashboard, multi-platform OAuth, automatic per-platform adaptation, and simple per-account pricing instead of per-post billing. A hard paywall from day one filters for people willing to pay. Packaging distribution as a REST API, CLI and MCP server lets AI assistants publish on the user's behalf, riding the 2026 agent-workflow wave. The founder built it for his own daily marketing, so the product matched a real pain from the start.

🛠️ 4-Step Operational Blueprint (SOP)

➔ Phase 01: Validate on your own accounts first. Post manually to 3-5 platforms daily for 30 days, log the time and format traps, interview 10 multi-platform creators about willingness to pay, and lock in one audience: short-form creators and solo founders.
➔ Phase 02: Ship an MVP in about a month. Supabase plus Vercel plus Trigger.dev plus Unkey keeps monthly infra under $2,000; connect X, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube OAuth and posting APIs first; adapt titles and covers per platform (the included postbridge_publish.py script runs caption adaptation and publish-queue checks); launch behind a hard paywall with Stripe per-account billing.
➔ Phase 03: Close the commercial loop. Price per connected account (Marketer from $39/mo, 15 accounts), unlimited posts on paid plans, then add REST API, npm CLI and MCP server so Claude and ChatGPT can schedule and publish for users, with usage tied to account count to stop abuse.
➔ Phase 04: Cold-start distribution. Build in public on X with real revenue updates, post short-form video daily from your own accounts, launch on Product Hunt and Indie Hackers, and give the first 100 paying users a founder price in exchange for testimonials and renewals.

⚠️ Pitfalls & Risk Warning

Distribution leans almost entirely on X, so an algorithm change or account problem stalls growth at once; build email and short-video backup channels from day one. Platform API policies (Meta, TikTok, Instagram) can tighten or reprice; failed posts need visible fallback and a retry queue, never silent drops. Solo operation means support, development and ops sit on one person; fix support flows before adding features once subscriptions grow. The ~1,600 subscriptions and 29,000+ signups are founder-reported snapshots; quote them with date and source, and re-check TrustMRR live figures before quoting.
