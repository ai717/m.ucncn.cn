🔍 Market Opportunity & Founder Bottlenecks

【Market Pain & Gap】Indie developers buy a domain for every side project, but none of them has email. Google Workspace charges $7 per mailbox per month, so 100 mailboxes cost $700/month. DNS records (MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC) are the step everyone puts off, and forwarding to Gmail can receive mail but cannot reply as the domain.
【Opportunity & Arbitrage】The break is to turn 'write DNS + create a mailbox + send mail' into one sentence: tell the agent, or run one command, and the records are written automatically at Cloudflare / Vercel / Porkbun, a real IMAP/SMTP mailbox is created, and mail goes out via Amazon SES. A $5 flat monthly price against $700/month per-mailbox pricing is itself the advertisement.

🛠️ 4-Step Operational Blueprint (SOP)

Phase 01 · Verify automatic DNS writing before selling anything Reverse Insight

Search Product Hunt comments and X for complaints about email for every domain, and confirm the buyer is someone with 3+ domains, not one. Test one domain on each of Cloudflare, Vercel and Porkbun, from creating the mailbox to receiving the first reply, end to end.

🏁 Deliverable:DNS records tested on 3 providers + a 1-page buyer profile
Phase 02 · Build only receiving, sending and DNS Rapid Build

The minimum version does three things only: write DNS automatically, create an IMAP/SMTP mailbox, and send via SES. Do not build newsletters or cold outreach. Limit new accounts to 50 sends per day to prevent abuse. Offer a CLI and an MCP entry point so an agent can run 'plainmail add hello@domain' for the user.

🏁 Deliverable:Working email service + CLI/MCP entry + 50 sends/day limit
Phase 03 · One price, first sale via Stripe Commercialization

Sell one price only: $5/month for 50 domains, 100 mailboxes and 25GB. Sign up with GitHub, cancel anytime. After Stripe fees of about 5% and about $30/month for hosting and SES, 80 users net about $340/month and 240 users net about $1,090/month. Do not copy per-mailbox pricing - that is the opponent you are beating.

🏁 Deliverable:Stripe $5/month subscription with the first sale
Phase 04 · Launch on Product Hunt and watch the cheap competitor Cold Outbound

Launch on Product Hunt with the price and the bundle in the headline. Post in indie developer communities asking how many domains people hold and how they receive mail today. Watch FranklyMail closely ($9/year, unlimited mailboxes and domains, about 1/7 of Plainmail's price); your selling points must be steadier SES deliverability, MCP/Claude integration and active maintenance, not price alone.

🏁 Deliverable:Product Hunt launch + a target of 80 paying users

⚠️ Pitfalls & Risk Warning

Watch three things. First, FranklyMail charges $9/year for unlimited mailboxes and domains, about 1/7 of Plainmail's price, so the price ceiling is low. Second, new accounts are limited to 50 sends a day and the product is positioned for mail between people, not newsletters or cold outreach - do not drift. Third, there is no revenue proof yet; $340-$1,090/month is a model built from user counts, not measured results.
