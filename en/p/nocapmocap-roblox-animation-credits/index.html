⚡ Institutional Due Diligence Verdict

Roblox creators genuinely lack animations and credit-pack cash flow is real, but motion quality and GPU cost are hard gates; it passes on the line and suits builders with 3D or motion expertise.

🔍 Market Opportunity & Founder Bottlenecks

Roblox indie developers need dozens of character animations per game, but hand-animating is slow and specialized, stock packs are expensive and overused (the founder calls it the Mixamo loop), and mocap suits are out of reach. The tool turns a text prompt or a phone video into R15 animation in seconds, previews it inside a free Studio plugin, sells prepaid credits that never expire ($20 for 100 credits), and even lets coding agents generate animations through an API. Copycats can target Unity, Godot or UEFN, or own one Roblox genre such as dance, fighting or parkour moves.

🛠️ 4-Step Operational Blueprint (SOP)

➔ Phase 01: Pick one engine (Roblox R15, Unity Humanoid or Godot) and one high-frequency move family; collect 50 animations target users actually ship and confirm skeleton and export standards.
➔ Phase 02: Build the pipeline: text-to-motion via open models or APIs, video via pose estimation plus retargeting; ship web generation with file export first, then an in-editor preview plugin. Quality floor: no foot sliding, no mesh clipping.
➔ Phase 03: Sell prepaid credit packs (never expire) plus monthly plan credits (expire monthly); quote the credit price before generating, bake GPU cost into pricing, and auto-refund failed generations.
➔ Phase 04: Cold-start with phone-video-to-character comparison clips on TikTok, YouTube and creator communities; list the free plugin on the official marketplace and watermark free exports for sharing.

⚠️ Pitfalls & Risk Warning

Motion quality is life or death: foot sliding, clipping and broken skeletons churn users instantly, so test inside real target games. Video mocap raises likeness and choreography rights issues; require users to only upload video they have rights to. GPU cost scales with generations, so price credits with margin and per-user caps. If the platform ships native AI animation, differentiate with vertical move libraries and workflow integration. Never-expiring credits are a liability; reserve compute budget for them.
