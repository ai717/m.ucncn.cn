🔍 Market Opportunity & Founder Bottlenecks

Tattoo artists spend about an hour hand-tracing each client photo into a transferable stencil, and in peak season that hour is a lost booking. Generic image generators produce pretty pictures, not clean, true-size, print-ready line art. A tool that does only photo-to-stencil well, with adjustable line weight and centimeter-accurate PDF export, priced by credits with a $0.99 trial pack, fits the workflow. Copycats should specialize in one style such as realism portraits.

🛠️ 4-Step Operational Blueprint (SOP)

➔ Phase 01: Interview 10 working tattoo artists and test stencil quality on their real photo samples until at least 8 say the output can go straight to the transfer printer.
➔ Phase 02: Build the pipeline: grayscale, contrast, edge extraction and binarization, exporting a line-art PNG plus a true-size PDF at 300 dpi with two sliders for line weight and detail.
➔ Phase 03: Charge by credits: Hobby $14 for 20, Pro $29 for 100, Studio from $69 for multi-seat shops, plus a one-time $0.99 five-credit trial; failed generations refund the credit.
➔ Phase 04: Launch in tattoo artist communities with before-and-after demos, hand tracing versus one click, and win the first 20 paying artists one by one.

⚠️ Pitfalls & Risk Warning

Line quality is existential: blurry edges, lost detail or wrong size get rejected instantly, so validate on real samples before launch. Client photos are portraits; state storage and deletion rules clearly and never train on them by default. The incumbent has been flat since July 2026, so growth must come from style depth and studio deals, not category tailwinds.
